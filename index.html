What is managed farmland near Bangalore? +

Managed farmland is agricultural land near Bangalore where a professional team handles everything — from plantation, maintenance, irrigation, and security — on your behalf. You own the land while we manage it for you, generating returns.

Are the plots legally verified? +

Yes, 100%. Every project at AnandaVan Farms goes through strict legal verification. We ensure clear titles, proper documentation, and full transparency throughout the purchase process.

What are the tax benefits of agricultural land? +

Agricultural income in India is exempt from income tax under Section 10(1) of the Income Tax Act. Also, under Section 54B, you can claim capital gains tax exemption when selling farmland and reinvesting in agricultural land.

Can I visit the farm before buying? +

Absolutely. We strongly encourage site visits before any purchase decision. You can book a free farm visit by filling the form on this page or calling us at +91 76194 59512.

What is the minimum size of farmland I can buy? +

We offer farmland plots starting from small fractions of an acre up to multiple acres, depending on the project and your budget. Our team can help you find the right size for your investment goals.
